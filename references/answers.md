# Drafting narrative answers

For motivation / fit / "tell us about yourself" style questions.

Structure:

1. One company- or role-specific motivation tied to something visible in the posting or on the company page you were given (product, problem, customers). If nothing specific is available, say so in the draft with `[?]` rather than inventing.
2. One or two concrete achievements from `Resume.md` / `Profile.md` that map to the most important requirements in the posting. Prefer shipped outcomes over tool lists.
3. If there is an obvious gap, one honest bridge: adjacent evidence + what is not yet demonstrated. Never imply experience the sources don't contain.

Length: ~100–150 words unless the form states a limit; respect character caps shown on the field.

Tone: direct, first person, no buzzword padding, no "I am writing to express my interest".

Always:

- Present every draft to the user before filling, in both modes, unless an `Answers/` note with `reuse: true` already covers the question.
- After approval, ask once: "Save to Answers/ for reuse?" Save with `{{company}}` / `{{role}}` placeholders where the company or role name appears.
- Placeholders: replace `{{company}}` and `{{role}}` from the posting before filling. If either is unknown, ask.

Never:

- Claim technologies, seniority, scale, or domain knowledge absent from the sources.
- Reuse a company-specific answer for a different company without re-templating it.
- Answer salary, availability, or legal questions here — those are hard stops (safety.md).
